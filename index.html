Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the LocalGemmaChat app for mobile devices (the "Application"), operated by BIN XIAO (the "Service Provider").

Your data stays on your device. LocalGemmaChat runs entirely on-device and contains no ad frameworks, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking technologies.

Information Collection and Use

  • The Service Provider does not collect, store, or receive any of your personal data. The Application has no account system, no analytics, and no servers.
  • Conversations are generated on your device by a local AI model.

What data is processed and where

  • Chat history and settings are stored only in the Application's local storage on your device. You can delete them at any time with the "Clear Chat" button, or by uninstalling the Application.
  • A local diagnostics log (capped at 512 KB) records memory and performance data to help troubleshoot issues. It stays on your device and is never transmitted.

Network access

The only network activity is downloading AI model files from Hugging Face directly to your device. This is a direct download from the model host; the Service Provider neither receives nor stores this traffic. All inference (generating responses) happens completely offline on your device.

Children

The Application is not intended for children under 16 years of age, and the Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any material changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
