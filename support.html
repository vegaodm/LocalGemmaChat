Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Is my data sent to a server?

A: No. LocalGemmaChat runs the AI model entirely on your device. Nothing you type is sent to any server.

Q: Does the app collect any data?

A: No. There is no account, no analytics, and no tracking. Chat history is stored only on your device and can be deleted with the "Clear Chat" button.

Q: Why does the app need to download a model before I can chat?

A: The model is downloaded once from Hugging Face (or hf-mirror.com). This direct download is the only network connection the app makes. After download, all generation is done offline on your device.

Q: How can I report a bug?

A: Please email us at vegaodm@outlook.com with your device model, iOS version, and a description of the issue.
